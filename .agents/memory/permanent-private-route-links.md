---
name: Permanent private route links
description: Product decision for sharing static routes privately without payment or an expiry window.
---

Private route sharing is a free, unlisted capability-link feature. A private route link remains available until its owner explicitly revokes it; it must not be presented as a paid, time-limited checkout product in the mobile route-sharing flow. After a link is created, the app may offer an optional $5 donation to help cover storage.

**Why:** The creator explicitly chose simple permanent sharing over a $19, 180-day private-project offer after checkout access failed before payment could begin.

**How to apply:** Use the private track-link sharing path for static route sharing. Preserve owner revocation and keep private links out of public/community discovery. Offer the suggested donation only after the link is available, via the general donation checkout, and never block sharing when it is declined or unavailable. Do not remove historical paid project data or change its access behavior without a separate request.
